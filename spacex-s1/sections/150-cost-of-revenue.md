Cost of revenue for the three months ended March 31, 2026 decreased by $16 million, or 5.4%, compared to the prior three months ended March 31, 2025. This decrease was primarily due to the decrease in customer launches and timing of work on government contracts of $34 million, offset by an increase of $10 million in inventory excess and obsolescence reserves and $10 million in launch hardware disposals for damaged Falcon fairings.
