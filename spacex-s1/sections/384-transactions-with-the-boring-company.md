In 2024, X entered into a lease for office space with a subsidiary owned by The Boring Company (an entity affiliated with Mr. Musk). Under this agreement, X made lease payments of $0.1 million in 2024, $1 million in 2025, and $0.1 million from January 1, 2026 through February 28, 2026. In addition, SpaceX incurred expenses of $1 million in 2025 in connection with the construction of tunnels by The Boring Company in Bastrop, Texas.
