The Company expenses the cost of advertising and other promotional expenditures to primarily market Starlink services as incurred. For the years ended December 31, 2025, 2024, and 2023, advertising expenses included in Selling, general, and administrative expenses on the consolidated statements of operations are $69 million, $31 million, and $29 million, respectively.
