Research and development for the three months ended March 31, 2026 increased by $82 million, or 66.7%, compared to the prior three months ended March 31, 2025. This increase was primarily due to higher costs for the next-generation production development of satellites of $62 million, Starlink Kits of $8 million, and ground equipment of $14 million.
