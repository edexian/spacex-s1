Revenue for the year ended December 31, 2025 increased by $4,659 million, or 33.2%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2024. This increase was primarily due to an increase in revenue from our Connectivity segment of $3,788 million as our Starlink Subscriber base continued to grow as well as our Connectivity enterprise and government sales, and increases in revenue from our Space segment of $290 million due to increases in Launch and

Development revenue for work performed on government contracts, and an increase in revenue from our AI segment of $581 million as advertising, Grok and X subscriptions, and data licensing arrangements grew.
