The Plan Administrator may amend, suspend or terminate the A&R 2024 Plan at any time; however certain enumerated material amendments may not be made without shareholder approval. Suspension or termination of the A&R 2024 Plan may not impair the rights and obligations of any outstanding Award. The Plan Administrator may also amend any outstanding Award, subject to the participant's consent in the event such amendment impairs such participant's rights under such Award. The A&R 2024 Plan is expected to be adopted by our board in connection with this offering and will terminate on December 10, 2034, unless earlier terminated by our board.
