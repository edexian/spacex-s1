Certain subsidiaries of xAI, have entered into certain equipment lease, sublease, and access agreements with Valor. These arrangements include (i) an equipment lease agreement under which a subsidiary of xAI leases computing and related equipment from Valor, which provides for aggregate cash payments of  $6,986 million to be made by such subsidiary over the life of the lease, (ii) a second equipment lease agreement under which such subsidiary leases certain computing and related equipment from Valor, which provides for aggregate cash payments of  $6,633 million to be made by such subsidiary over the life of the lease, and (iii) a third equipment lease under which such subsidiary leases certain computing and related equipment from Valor, which provides for aggregate cash payments of $6,587 million to be made by such subsidiary over the life of the lease. The lessees' payments and performance obligations under these agreements are guaranteed by Space Exploration Technologies Corp. or one of its subsidiaries. Pursuant to the lease agreements described above, our subsidiaries have made payments of $885 million in 2025, and $857 million from January 1, 2026 through February 28, 2026.

In connection with certain X API services, X received payments from Valor of  $1 million in 2024, $1 million in 2025, and $0.1 million from January 1, 2026 through February 28, 2026.
