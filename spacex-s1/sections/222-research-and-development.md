Research and development for the year ended December 31, 2024 increased by $72 million, or 18.8%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2023. This increase was primarily due to higher costs for the next-generation production development of satellites of $73 million, ground equipment of $4 million, offset by lower costs of $4 million for Starlink Kits.
