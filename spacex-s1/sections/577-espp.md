During the years ended December 31, 2025, 2024, and 2023, under the ESPP, the Company issued 6.3 million, 8.0 million and 6.5 million shares, respectively. For the year ended December 31, 2025, the Company issued 0.2 million shares under the NQ ESPP. No shares were issued under NQ ESPP during the years ended December 31, 2024 and 2023.
