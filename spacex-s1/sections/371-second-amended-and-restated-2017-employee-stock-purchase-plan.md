In connection with this offering, we intend to further amend and restated our 2017 ESPP. The purpose of the A&R 2017 ESPP is to encourage and enable our eligible employees to acquire a proprietary interest in us through the ownership of our Class A common stock. The A&R 2017 ESPP, and the rights of participants to make purchases thereunder, is intended to qualify under the provisions of Section 423 of the Code.

The following description of the A&R 2017 ESPP is not intended to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the complete text of the A&R 2017 ESPP, a copy of which will be filed as an exhibit to the registration statement of which this prospectus forms a part. Please read the A&R 2017 ESPP in its entirety.
