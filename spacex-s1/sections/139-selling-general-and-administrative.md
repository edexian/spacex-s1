Selling, general, and administrative expense for the three months ended March 31, 2026 increased by $253 million, or 51.3%, compared to the prior three months ended March 31, 2025. This increase was primarily due to higher employee-related costs and professional fees for our AI segment of $163 million as our AI business grew rapidly, higher marketing and international expansion costs of $79 million and $23 million, respectively, for our Connectivity segment. These increases were partially offset by lower expenses of $18 million in our Space segment.
