In the event of a liquidation, dissolution, or winding up of the Company, upon the completion of the distributions required with respect to the SpaceX Redeemable Convertible Preferred Stock, if assets remain in the Company, the then remaining assets will be distributed on an equal priority, pro rata basis to the holders of SpaceX Common Stock.
