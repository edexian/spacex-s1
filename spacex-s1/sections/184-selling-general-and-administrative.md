Selling, general, and administrative for the year ended December 31, 2025 decreased by $26 million, or 6.9%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2024. This decrease was primarily due to lower allocated general and administrative overhead of $52 million, partially offset by higher employee compensation expenses (including salaries, benefits, and share-based compensation) of $16 million.
