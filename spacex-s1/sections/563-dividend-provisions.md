Subject to the prior rights of holders of all classes and series of stock at the time outstanding having prior rights as to dividends, holders of SpaceX Common Stock shall be entitled to receive, when, as and if declared by the Board of Directors, out of any funds legally available, such dividends as may be declared from time to time by the Board of Directors. For the period from inception through December 31, 2025, no dividends were declared on SpaceX Common Stock.
