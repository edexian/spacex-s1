As of December 31, 2025 and 2024, the Company held investments in unconsolidated affiliates which are accounted for as equity investments without readily determinable fair values of $157 million and $154 million, respectively.

For the years ended December 31, 2025, 2024, and 2023, the Company recorded a total of $0 million, $1 million, and $45 million of impairment charges related to the equity method investments in Other income (expense), net in the consolidated statements of operations. The Company recorded cumulative downward adjustments of $59 million on these investments as of December 31, 2025. No upward adjustments were recorded in the years ended December 31, 2025, 2024 and 2023.
