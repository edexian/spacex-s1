Subject to the prior rights of holders of all classes and series of the Company's capital stock at the time outstanding having prior rights as to dividends, the holders of shares of Class A common stock, Class B common stock and Class C common stock will be entitled to receive such dividends as may be declared from time to time by the board. Any dividends paid to the holders of shares of Class A common stock, Class B common stock and Class C common stock will be paid pro rata, on an equal priority, pari passu basis.
