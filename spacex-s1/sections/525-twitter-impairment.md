In 2023, the Company rebranded its Twitter platform to X. As a result of the rebranding, the Company performed an impairment assessment and recorded an impairment charge of $3,775 million on its previously indefinite-lived brand intangible for the AI segment. The Company's brand intangible asset was determined to no longer be indefinitelived and is presented as a finite-lived intangible asset with a five-year useful life. The fair value of the brand intangible asset was determined using the relief-from-royalty method.
