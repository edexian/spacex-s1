We estimate that our satellites have useful lives of three to five years based on engineering studies, historical onorbit performance, propellant life, utilization patterns, design enhancements across generations, and planned transitions to newer satellite technology. We, however, often deorbit satellites before the end of their useful lives, primarily to reduce degradation risks that could impair our autonomous collision avoidance system and compromise constellation safety. To date, our autonomous collision avoidance system has not experienced any failures resulting in satellite loss, and satellite losses from other causes remain de minimis.
