The Company recognizes investment tax credits when there is reasonable assurance that the credit will be received and the Company will comply with the conditions specified in the agreement or statutory requirements. The Company records capital-related credits as a reduction to Property, plant, and equipment, net within the consolidated balance sheets and recognizes a reduction to depreciation expense over the useful life of the corresponding acquired asset.
