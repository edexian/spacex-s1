On March 28, 2025, xAI adopted an Amended and Restated Articles of Incorporation, which established its capital structure and designated multiple classes of common stock and several series of redeemable convertible preferred stock. The Articles were subsequently amended and restated through January 30, 2026 (collectively, the 'xAI Articles of Incorporation') to add and authorize additional series of redeemable convertible preferred stock with no economic changes to any previously existing series.
