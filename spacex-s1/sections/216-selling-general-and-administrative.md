Selling, general, and administrative for the year ended December 31, 2024 increased by $24 million, or 7.0%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2023. This increase was primarily due to higher employee compensation expenses (including salaries, benefits, and share-based compensation) and professional fees of $25 million.
