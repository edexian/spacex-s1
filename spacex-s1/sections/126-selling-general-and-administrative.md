AI segment's SG&A expenses consist primarily of employee compensation expenses (including salaries, benefits, and share-based compensation) for our sales, sales support, marketing, finance, legal, information technology, human resources and other administrative employees. In addition, SG&A expenses include fees and costs for professional services, including consulting, content moderation, third-party legal and accounting services and facilities costs and other supporting overhead costs that are not allocated to other departments.

We expect AI SG&A to increase in absolute dollars to support growth of our business, and to decrease as a percentage of revenue as we continue to work to reduce operating costs as a percentage of revenue. Additionally, AI SG&A may increase as a result of the compute agreement with Cursor.
