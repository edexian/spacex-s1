As of March 31, 2026 and December 31, 2025, the Company held investments in unconsolidated affiliates which are accounted for as equity investments without readily determinable fair values of $168 million and $157 million, respectively. The Company recorded cumulative downward adjustments of $59 million on these investments as of March 31, 2026. Upward adjustments or impairment on these investments during the three months ended March 31, 2026 and 2025 were not material.
