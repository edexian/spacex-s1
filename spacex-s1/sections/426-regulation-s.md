Regulation S under the Securities Act ('Regulation S') provides that ordinary shares owned by any person may be sold without registration in the United States, provided that the sale is effected in an offshore transaction and no directed selling efforts are made in the United States (as these terms are defined in Regulation S), subject to certain other conditions. In general, this means that our Class A common stock may be sold outside the United States under certain circumstances without registration in the United States being required.
