Research and development for the year ended December 31, 2024 increased by $297 million, or 19.3%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2023. This increase was primarily due to higher production costs of $159 million, higher launch costs of $67 million, and higher engineering costs of $56 million due to the increased investment in the development of the Starship vehicle and related launch facilities.
