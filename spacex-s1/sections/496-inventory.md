Inventory consists primarily of raw materials and work-in-progress used in the production of launch vehicles and Starlink Kits, and finished goods for Starlink Kits, Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy second stages awaiting launch.

Inventory is computed using standard cost or weighted average, which approximates actual cost on a first-in, firstout basis and is stated at the lower of cost or net realizable value. The Company records inventory write-downs in Cost of revenue in the consolidated statements of operations for estimated obsolescence or unmarketable inventories based upon assumptions about future demand and design, and technological or other changes.
