Cost of revenue for the year ended December 31, 2024 increased by $32 million, or 1.9%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2023. This increase was primarily due to higher server depreciation of $97 million, partially offset by lower infrastructure and revenue share expenses of $46 million, and lower employee and facilities-related expenses of $18 million resulting from the Company's restructuring and cost reduction efforts.
