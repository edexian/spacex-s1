The Company had outstanding letters of credit of $517 million at March 31, 2026 related to various customer contracts, insurance agreements, and facility lease agreements. All of the outstanding letters of credit were collateralized by restricted cash. The Company also had surety bonds of $447 million for self-insured workers' compensation programs and other governmental licenses at March 31, 2026.
