All of our U.S. employees, including our NEOs, are eligible to participate in our 401(k) plan, which is a broadbased, tax-qualified defined contribution retirement plan. Under the 401(k) plan, we may make discretionary matching and non-elective contributions, subject to certain limits under the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (the 'Code'), and such contributions would vest ratably and would be 100% vested after five years of credited service; however, no such company contributions were made for 2025.
