Our bylaws will specify that the required ownership threshold for a shareholder or group of shareholders to institute or maintain a derivative proceeding in the right of the Company for purposes of Section 21.552(a)(3) of the TBOC will be 3% of the outstanding shares of common stock of the Company. This provision will continue to apply so long as any shares of the Company's common stock are listed for trading on a national securities exchange or the Company affirmatively elects to be governed by TBOC 21.419 and has 500 or more shareholders.
