Cash and cash equivalents consist of cash in checking accounts, money market accounts, and certificates of deposit at high quality financial institutions primarily in the U.S. All highly liquid investments with an original maturity of three months or less at the date of purchase are considered to be cash equivalents. The Company maintains certain cash and cash equivalents for which the withdrawal or use is restricted. The restricted cash and cash equivalents are generally held in separate, dedicated accounts required to secure letters of credit related to various customer, insurance, and facility lease agreements.
