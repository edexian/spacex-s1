Cost of revenue for the year ended December 31, 2024 decreased by $128 million, or 7.6%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2023. This decrease was primarily due to increased reusability of our Falcon launch vehicles resulting in lower depreciation of $80 million, lowering the cost of each launch. The decrease was also due to the relative increase in Starlink satellite launches from 63 launches in 2023 to 89 launches in 2024, resulting in relatively more of our launch operations and overhead costs capitalized in our Connectivity segment of $99 million. This decrease was offset by an increase in launch overhead costs of $77 million due to the increase in Falcon launches.
