The A&R 2024 Plan will be administered by our board or a committee thereof designated by our board to administer the A&R 2024 Plan, which we refer to herein as the 'Plan Administrator.' The Plan Administrator will have broad authority, subject to the provisions of the A&R 2024 Plan, to administer and interpret the A&R 2024 Plan and Awards granted thereunder. All decisions and actions of the Plan Administrator will be final, binding and conclusive on all persons.
