Benefit from income taxes for the year ended December 31, 2024 increased by $186 million, or 51.2%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2023. This increase was primarily due to the change in the realizability of our net deferred tax assets. As of December 31, 2024, we forecasted additional deferred tax assets related to U.S. R&D credits would be utilized.
