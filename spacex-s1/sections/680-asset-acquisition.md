On April 30, 2026, the Company entered into an asset purchase agreement with an unaffiliated third party to purchase certain mobile gas turbines and related packages for approximately $2,000 million (the 'Turbine Acquisition'). The closing of the Turbine Acquisition is expected to occur in May 2026 and is subject to customary closing conditions. The seller has also agreed to enter into a post-closing services agreement to support the Company's turbine operations. The Turbine Acquisition will help provide power to the Company's data centers.
