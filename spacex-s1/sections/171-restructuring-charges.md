Restructuring charges for the year ended December 31, 2025 increased by $274 million, or 128.6%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2024.  This increase was primarily due to additional expense related to the settlement to former Twitter employees as part of the workforce reduction program implemented in 2022.
