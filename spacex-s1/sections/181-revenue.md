Revenue for the year ended December 31, 2025 increased by $290 million, or 7.6%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2024.  Launch Services revenue remained relatively flat year over year, while Launch and Development revenue increased by $298 million.  The Launch and Development revenue increase was primarily driven by increased revenue for an extended contract with NASA for additional Cargo Resupply Services (CRS) missions to the International Space Station and increased revenue from a U.S. Department of War contract. While total Falcon launches increased by 31 from 134 in 2024 to 165 in 2025, Space customer launches and average price per launch remained relatively flat year over year.
