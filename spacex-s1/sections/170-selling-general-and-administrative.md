Selling, general, and administrative expense for the year ended December 31, 2025 increased by $831 million, or 45.8%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2024. This increase was primarily due to higher employee and facilities-related costs and higher legal expenses for our AI segment of $722 million as our AI business grew rapidly, and higher marketing and international expansion costs of $53 million and $37 million, respectively, for our Connectivity segment. These increases were partially offset by lower allocated general and administrative overhead in our Space segment.
