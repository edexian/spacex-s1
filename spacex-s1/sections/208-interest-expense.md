Interest expense for the year ended December 31, 2024 decreased by $113 million, or 6.7%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2023. This decrease was primarily due to the debt issuance costs related to the X Bridge Credit Facilities being amortized only through July 2024, the original maturity date, as compared to a full year of amortization in 2023.
