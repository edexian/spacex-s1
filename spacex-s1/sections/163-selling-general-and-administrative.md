Selling, general, and administrative for the three months ended March 31, 2026 increased by $163 million, or 54.3%, compared to the prior three months ended March 31, 2025. This increase was primarily due to higher employee compensation expenses (including salaries, benefits, and share-based compensation) of $148 million as we continue to expand our AI business and higher legal expenses of $33 million, partially offset by a decrease in facilities and general and administrative costs of $18 million.
