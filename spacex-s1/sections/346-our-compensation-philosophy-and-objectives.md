Our compensation program is designed to attract, retain and reward executives and employees, with a heavy emphasis on equity compensation to provide employees with a financial stake in our business and an ownership mindset. We offer a number of programs that allow employees to voluntarily elect to receive elements of their compensation in equity or to otherwise increase their ownership interests in the Company.
