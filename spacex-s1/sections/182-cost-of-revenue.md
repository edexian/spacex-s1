Cost of revenue for the year ended December 31, 2025 decreased by $189 million, or 12.2%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2024. This decrease was primarily due increased reusability of our Falcon launch vehicles resulting in lower deprecation of $240 million, lowering the cost of each launch, and lower overhead costs of $11 million. The decrease is also due to the relative increase in Starlink satellite launches from 89 launches in 2024 to 122 launches in 2025, resulting in relatively more of our launch operations and overhead costs capitalized in our Connectivity segment of $14 million.  This decrease was partially offset by an increase in inventory excess and obsolescence reserves of $51 million mainly due to less demand on rocket vehicle and spacecraft parts as reusability has increased.
