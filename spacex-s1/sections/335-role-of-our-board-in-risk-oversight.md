We face a number of risks, including those described under the section titled 'Risk Factors' included elsewhere in this prospectus. Our board believes that risk management is an important part of establishing, updating and executing on our business strategy. Our board, as a whole and at the committee level, has oversight responsibility relating to risks that could affect our corporate strategy, business objectives, compliance, operations and financial condition and performance. Our board focuses its oversight on the most significant risks facing us and on the processes to identify, prioritize, assess, manage and mitigate those risks. While our board has an oversight role, management is principally tasked with direct responsibility for management and assessment of risks and the implementation of processes and controls to mitigate their effects on us.
