Restructuring charges for the year ended December 31, 2024 decreased by $24 million, or 10.1%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2023.  This decrease was due to the impairment on office leases assumed as part of the Twitter acquisition that occurred during the year ended December 31, 2023, partially offset by an increase in workforce-related restructuring charges.
