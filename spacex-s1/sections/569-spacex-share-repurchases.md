During the year ended December 31, 2025, SpaceX repurchased $522 million or 14.0 million shares of SpaceX Common Stock from eligible current and former employees. Similarly, the Company repurchased $920 million or 38.7 million shares of SpaceX Common Stock from eligible current and former employees and existing shareholders during the year ended December 31, 2024, as well as $101 million or 0.1 million shares of SpaceX Redeemable Convertible Preferred Stock in a number of unrelated transactions with existing shareholders at their then-current fair market value. The Company only repurchased shares held by eligible participants for more than six months at a purchase price per share equal to the then current fair market value.

All SpaceX shares repurchased to date have been retired.
