Upon the effective date of the xAI Merger, every outstanding share of xAI Common Stock, whether Class A, Class B, or Limited Voting, converted into the right to receive SpaceX Common Stock at a fixed exchange ratio of 0.1433 SpaceX shares per share of xAI Common Stock (on a pre-2026 Stock Split basis), unless the holder was an eligible service provider and elected to receive cash of $75.46 per share of xAI Class A or Class B. No fractional SpaceX shares were issued and all share amounts were rounded up to the nearest whole number. Any shares of xAI Common Stock previously held by the Company were canceled and retired and did not receive any consideration.
