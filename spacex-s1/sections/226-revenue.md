Revenue for the year ended December 31, 2024 decreased by $341 million, or 11.5%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2023. This decrease was due to a decrease in advertising revenue of $595 million, partially offset by an increase in AI solutions and infrastructure revenue of $254 million. The decrease in advertising revenue was due to the loss of advertising partners for X. The increase in AI solutions and infrastructure was due to an increase in X subscription revenue of $157 million and an increase in data licensing arrangements of $90 million.  In 2023 and 2024, substantially all of our AI segment revenue consisted of advertising, subscriptions, and data licensing revenue generated from X, formerly known as Twitter.
