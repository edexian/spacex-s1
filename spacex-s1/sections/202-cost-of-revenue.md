Cost of revenue for the year ended December 31, 2024 increased by $1,886 million, or 30.9%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2023. This increase was primarily due to a higher cost of revenue from the Connectivity segment of $1,982 million as a result of the higher volume spend on Starlink Kits as deliveries increased and higher depreciation of launch costs driven by an increase in the number of satellites placed into orbit, partially offset by cost efficiency from increased reusability of our Falcon launch vehicles in our Space segment of $128 million.
