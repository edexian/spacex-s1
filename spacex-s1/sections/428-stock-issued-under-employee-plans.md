We intend to file a registration statement on Form S-8 under the Securities Act to register stock issuable under our A&R 2024 Plan and A&R 2017 ESPP and to register stock issuable pursuant to outstanding awards under our other Equity Plans. This registration statement on Form S-8 is expected to be filed following the effective date of the registration statement of which this prospectus is a part and will be effective immediately upon filing. Accordingly, shares of Class A common stock registered under such registration statement will be available for sale in the open market following the effective date, unless such shares are subject to vesting restrictions with us or the lock-up restrictions described above.
