Interest expense for the year ended December 31, 2025 increased by $365 million, or 23.1%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2024. This increase was primarily due to new term loans and senior notes entered into by the Company and other financing arrangements for GPUs entered into during the year by our AI segment.
