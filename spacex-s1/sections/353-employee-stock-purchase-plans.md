Historically, we have provided two employee stock purchase plans in which all of our U.S. employees, including the NEOs, are eligible to participate. Our Amended and Restated 2017 Employee Stock Purchase Plan (the '2017 ESPP') is intended to qualify under Section 423 of the Code and allows eligible employees to purchase shares of Class C common stock using accumulated payroll contributions at a discount. It is expected that the 2017 ESPP will be amended and restated in connection with this offering, as described below. Our 2023 Non-Qualified ESPP (the 'NQ ESPP') is not intended to qualify under Section 423 of the Code and allows eligible employees to purchase shares of Class C common stock using accumulated payroll contributions at fair market value. Our NQ ESPP will be discontinued in connection with this offering.
