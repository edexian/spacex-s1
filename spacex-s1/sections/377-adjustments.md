In the event of any reorganizations, recapitalizations, stock splits, reverse stock splits, stock dividends, extraordinary dividends or distributions, or similar events, the ESPP Administrator will appropriately adjust the number and class of shares available under the A&R 2017 ESPP and subject to the purchase limits under each ongoing offering and the applicable purchase price of such shares in each ongoing offering.
