Selling, general, and administrative for the three months ended March 31, 2026 increased by $108 million, or 102.9%, compared to the prior three months ended March 31, 2025. This increase was primarily driven by higher marketing costs of $79 million and higher international expansion costs of $23 million, partially offset by lower bad debt expense of $9 million.
