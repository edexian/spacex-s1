Deferred revenue is recorded when cash payments are received or due, in advance of the Company's performance. Deferred revenue primarily relates to Space agreements and Connectivity enterprise and government contracts. Total deferred revenue as of December 31, 2025 was $12,116 million, of which $1,165 million was recognized as revenue for the three months ended March 31, 2026. Total deferred revenue as of March 31, 2026 was $13,236 million.
