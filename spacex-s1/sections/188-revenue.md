Revenue for the year ended December 31, 2025 increased by $3,788 million, or 49.8%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2024. This increase was primarily driven by an increase of $2,377 million in revenue from our consumer subscribers, composed of 99.9% growth in Starlink Subscribers, offset by an 11.2% decline in Starlink Subscriber ARPU, primarily due to international expansion and the addition of lower priced service plans. In addition, Connectivity revenue had an increase of $1,411 million from our enterprise and government customers, primarily driven by the growth in our enterprise connectivity business of $1,218 million inclusive of growth in our mobile connectivity business of $632 million, and growth in our government connectivity business of $193 million.
