General. In April 2024 and amended through March 2026, a subsidiary of xAI, an indirect subsidiary of the Company, entered into a revolving line of credit up to borrowing capacity of $250 million.  The Company had no borrowings under the line of credit during the three months ended March 31, 2026 and 2025.

Interest Rates. Interest on any borrowings is calculated based on the 30-day average SOFR plus the International Swaps and Derivatives Association spread adjustment plus a spread of 40 basis points.

Guarantors and Collateral. The agreement permits borrowings up to the value of the pledged collateral held in custody, less any outstanding loan balances, accrued interest, and fees. The pledged collateral consisted of securities held in xAI's custodial account.
