The following examples illustrate ways in which customers across a range of industries have used and benefited from our solutions within our Space, Connectivity, and AI segments. These examples are intended to highlight representative applications of our offerings and the types of operational, performance and efficiency benefits that customers may realize.

In addition, we include examples of our deployment of Starlink services in response to natural disasters, which demonstrate our ability to rapidly establish communications infrastructure to support emergency response and recovery efforts in challenging environments.
