Impairment for the three months ended March 31, 2026 decreased by $24 million compared to the prior three months ended March 31, 2025. This decrease was primarily due to a non-recurring impairment loss on a Falcon 9 booster due to a post-landing anomaly during the three months ended March 31, 2025. There was no impairment for the three months ended March 31, 2026.
