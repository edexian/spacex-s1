Revenue for the three months ended March 31, 2026 increased by $782 million, or 31.6%, compared to the prior three months ended March 31, 2025. This increase was primarily driven by an increase of $656 million in revenue from our consumer subscribers, composed of 104.7% growth in Starlink Subscribers, offset by an 22.9% decline in Starlink Subscriber ARPU, primarily due to international expansion and the addition of lower priced service plans. In addition, enterprise and government revenue had an increase of $126 million primarily driven by the growth in our aviation, maritime, and other enterprise business of $209 million, our mobile connectivity business of $85 million, partially offset by a decrease of $175 million in our government connectivity business.
