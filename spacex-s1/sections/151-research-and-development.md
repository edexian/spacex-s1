Research and development for the three months ended March 31, 2026 increased by $404 million, or 76.8%, compared to the prior three months ended March 31, 2025. This increase was primarily driven by higher production costs of $194 million, higher engineering costs of $95 million, and higher test and launch costs of $62 million, due to the accelerated investment in development of the Starship vehicle and continued development of production and launch facilities to support future Starship launches.
