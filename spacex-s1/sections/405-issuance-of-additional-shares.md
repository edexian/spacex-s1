We may issue additional authorized shares of Class A common stock, Class B common stock and Class C common stock at any time or from time to time, subject to applicable provisions of our charter, our bylaws and Texas law. Our charter will provide that additional shares of Class B common stock may only be issued in the future to Mr. Musk, his family members and certain entities permitted under our charter.
