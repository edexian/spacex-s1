Selling, general, and administrative for the year ended December 31, 2025 increased by $135 million, or 40.4%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2024. This increase was primarily driven by higher marketing costs of $53 million, higher international expansion costs of $37 million, and higher allocated general and administrative overhead of $67 million.
