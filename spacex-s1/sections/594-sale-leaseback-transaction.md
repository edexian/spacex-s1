In January 2026, and as further amended on February 18, 2026, CTC entered into an equipment lease agreement with Valor for certain AI infrastructure hardware ('Valor transaction II '). Similar to the Valor transaction, the Valor transaction II was considered to be a transaction with a related party. The Valor transaction II is deemed to be a failed sale-leaseback transaction and the Company recorded the related debt of $5,365 million in the Company's consolidated balance sheets.
