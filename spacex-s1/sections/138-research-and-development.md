Research and development expense for the three months ended March 31, 2026 increased by $1,957 million, or 125.7%, compared to the prior three months ended March 31, 2025. This increase was primarily due to higher costs in our AI segment of $1,471 million driven by depreciation of GPU hardware, and the cost of cloud computing and data center infrastructure expenses as a result of our AI data center expansions and higher costs from our Space segment of $404 million driven by accelerated investment in our Starship vehicle and related facilities.
