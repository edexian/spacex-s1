The A&R 2017 ESPP will be administered by our board or a committee thereof designated by our board to administer the A&R 2017 ESPP, which we refer to herein as the 'ESPP Administrator.' The ESPP Administrator has the final power to determine all questions of policy and expediency that may arise in the administration of the A&R 2017 ESPP. The ESPP Administrator may delegate its responsibilities under the A&R 2017 ESPP to one or more other persons.
