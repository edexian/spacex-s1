The Company had outstanding letters of credit of $348 million at December 31, 2025 related to various customer contracts, insurance agreements, and facility lease agreements. All of the outstanding letters of credit were collateralized by restricted cash. The Company also had surety bonds of $51 million for self-insured workers' compensation programs and other governmental licenses at December 31, 2025.
