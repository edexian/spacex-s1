AI segment's R&D expenses mainly relate to the training of Grok, our leading frontier model, development, build, and testing of our next-generation AI-enabled products and data center costs to train AI-enabled products. These costs include cloud computing expenses, employee compensation expenses (including salaries, benefits, and sharebased compensation), power generation costs, and depreciation of data center assets, including processors, equipment lease expenses, and networking equipment.

We expect AI R&D expenses to increase, both in absolute dollars and as a percentage of revenue, as we invest in compute infrastructure for Grok. Additionally, AI R&D expenses may increase as a result  of the compute agreement with Cursor.
