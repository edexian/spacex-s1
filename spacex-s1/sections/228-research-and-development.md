Research and development for the year ended December 31, 2024 increased by $990 million, or 531.5%, compared to the prior year ended December 31, 2023. This increase was primarily due to increased investments made in advancing our AI technologies, including employee compensation expenses (including salaries, benefits, and sharebased compensation) and infrastructure services of $703 million and higher depreciation of $321 million for our equipment hardware.
