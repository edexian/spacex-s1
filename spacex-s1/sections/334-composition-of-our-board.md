Upon the consummation of the offering, our board will consist of eight directors. Subject to the terms of our charter and bylaws, the number of directors on our board will be determined from time to time by our board. Under the terms of our charter, the holders of our outstanding Class B common stock, voting separately as a class, will have the right to elect 51% of the total number of authorized directors, rounded up to the nearest whole number (the 'Class B Directors'). Holders of Class A and Class B common stock, voting together as a single class, will elect the remaining members of our board (the 'Common Stock Directors').
